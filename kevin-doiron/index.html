Meet Kevin Doiron

Capital Heights knowledge.
An eye for potential.

Kevin Doiron brings his connection with J.T. Doiron Realtors in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, to his work with Geaux Home Real Estate. His close knowledge of Capital Heights and focus on renovation projects and investment properties offer clients another perspective on what a property can become.

For a buyer considering a home that needs work, a seller preparing a property for its next chapter, or an investor exploring an opportunity, Kevin starts with a conversation about the property and the plan behind it.

The J.T. Doiron connection

A lasting Capital Heights story.

Kevin’s connection to J.T. Doiron Realtors is part of a longer neighborhood history. In 2012, the Foundation for Historical Louisiana recognized Michael Doiron for neighborhood preservation, citing more than 40 homes moved and restored, plus 10 others built in Capital Heights.

Kevin brings his own close knowledge of Capital Heights to Geaux Home, helping clients consider a property’s character, renovation possibilities and fit with their plans.

Historical recognition of Michael Doiron, reported by Business Report, July 17, 2012. Read the preservation story.
