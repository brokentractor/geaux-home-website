Lacy Crouch

Broker / Owner · Geaux Home Real Estate

Local roots.
A personal commitment.

Born and raised in Baton Rouge, I’ve spent over 20 years helping clients navigate the Louisiana real estate market with honesty, hustle, and heart. Real estate isn’t just my profession—it’s personal.

I earned my undergraduate degree from Centenary College of Louisiana, where I played Division I softball. I was inducted into the Centenary Athletic Hall of Fame, a reminder that being the “little guy” doesn’t mean you can’t win big. It just means you find your edge.

That’s exactly how I approach real estate. I’ve built Geaux Home around that same underdog spirit—scrappy, creative, and strategic. Whether it’s finding the right home, outmaneuvering a crowded market, or negotiating with care and confidence, I help my clients find their advantage.

I also earned a Master of Divinity from Southern Methodist University, which shaped my values and strengthened my commitment to serving others—with clarity, compassion, and purpose.

From Mid City and Garden District to Rouzan and University Club, I represent clients throughout Baton Rouge and across southern Louisiana.
