Meet Jody Hammett

A thoughtful eye.
A people-first approach.

For Jody Hammett, real estate is about people first—understanding their needs, communicating clearly, and helping them move forward with confidence.

Based in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, Jody brings more than 30 years of business experience and a deep commitment to building lasting relationships. His approachable style helps clients feel comfortable, informed, and supported throughout the entire process.

Jody has a true appreciation for Baton Rouge and the unique character of each neighborhood. He believes every client has their own story, lifestyle, and vision—and he’s passionate about helping match people with spaces that truly fit how they want to live.

With more than 15 years of experience in design and home renovation, Jody offers clients a trained eye for both detail and potential. Buyers benefit from his ability to envision possibilities within a space, while sellers receive thoughtful guidance on preparing and presenting their homes for today’s market.

Jody also holds a PhD in Human Resource Education and Workforce Development, strengthening his ability to understand people, communicate effectively, and guide clients through important decisions with clarity and trust.

Known for his integrity, responsiveness, and steady guidance, Jody believes success is measured by the relationships built along the way.
