Real estate should feel personal.

Founded and led by broker Lacy Crouch, Geaux Home is a boutique real estate brokerage based in Baton Rouge, serving clients across south Louisiana with a commitment to clarity, strategy, and heart.

With over 20 years of experience in residential real estate, Lacy brings deep local knowledge and sharp negotiation skills to every deal. Born and raised in Baton Rouge, she knows the nuances of neighborhoods from Mid City to University Club, from Garden District to Prairieville and beyond.

Whether you’re buying your first home, selling a property you’ve loved, or relocating to Baton Rouge, you’ll get a trusted advisor, fierce advocate, and deeply rooted local ready to help you land in the right place—on your terms.
