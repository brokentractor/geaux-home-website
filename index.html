I’m Lacy. After more than 20 years in Louisiana real estate, I built Geaux Home around a simple idea: your next move deserves personal attention.

From a first home to a fresh start, we bring local knowledge, honest conversations, and a thoughtful plan. Baton Rouge is home, and we’re here to help you find your place in it.

Geaux Home Real Estate (pronounced “Go Home”) is a boutique real estate brokerage based in Baton Rouge, Louisiana. Whether you’re buying your first home, selling a place you’ve loved, or moving to the Baton Rouge area, our team is here to help you plan your next move.

Where do I start if I’m thinking about buying?

Start with a conversation about your timing, budget, and what you want home to feel like. We’ll help you make a plan and decide which neighborhoods and homes to explore.

Talk about buying →
How do I arrange a home tour?

Send us the address or listing link and a few times that work for you. We’ll check availability and coordinate the next steps with you.

Ask about a home →
What should I do before putting my home on the market?

Let’s walk through your home and talk about your goals first. Together, we can decide which preparation, presentation, and pricing choices make sense before you commit to improvements.
