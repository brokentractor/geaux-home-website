Privacy notice

How Geaux Home Real Estate LLC handles information you share through this website.

Information you give us

When you send our contact form, we receive what you enter: your name, email address, phone number, what you’re interested in, a property address or area, and your message. The form is processed by our website host, Netlify, and delivered to Lacy Crouch by email.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry and help with your real estate needs. We don’t sell your information, and we won’t add you to marketing emails or texts unless you ask us to.

Calls and texts

If you call or text our office at (225) 338-8414, your carrier’s standard rates apply. We keep records of our conversations as needed to serve you and run our business.

Videos and other websites

Video thumbnails on this site load from YouTube’s image service. Pressing play loads YouTube’s player, which is subject to Google’s privacy policy. Links to Instagram, Facebook, Google reviews, and property search services take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

This website doesn’t use advertising pixels or analytics tracking. Our host may record standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to run and protect the site.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep inquiries as long as needed to serve you and meet business and legal record-keeping requirements. To ask what we have or to request that we delete it, email lacy@geauxhomerealestate.com.
