Draft privacy notice

This notice describes the current private preview and must be updated before live lead collection.

Information in this preview

The contact form prepares an email draft in your own email app or copies text when you request it. This preview does not submit the form to a server, save contact records, or connect to a CRM.

The owner’s launch checklist uses this browser’s local storage to remember checked items. That checklist is not synchronized across devices.

This draft does not install advertising pixels or analytics. The silent background film is served directly by this website and has no audio track. It can be paused with its on-screen control. Reduced-motion and data-saving preferences use a still unless you choose to play it. Film preview thumbnails are requested from YouTube’s image service. Clicking a YouTube play button loads an embedded YouTube player, which is subject to Google’s policies. A direct YouTube link is also provided. Hosting and private access are provided through ChatGPT Sites, which may process technical access information under its own policies. External links and your email provider have their own policies.

Before public launch

A final notice must identify the business contact details and the actual form, CRM, IDX, analytics, communication, retention, and consent practices selected for the live site.
